                                                                                                                                       | How likely the record is the person it was found for, from 0.01 to 0.99, to two places. A record found by the person's own email or phone starts as theirs, and one found by a search of a value another record holds starts at that record's likelihood divided by one plus the records its search found on its site; any other starts lower the more people its search found on its site, or anywhere for a name search. Each value it shares with what is known of the person raises it, a rare value more than a common one, and a different birth date or full name lowers it. What is known is what the person's row gives and the values of their joined records: those found by their email or phone, those a reviewer joined and those whose likelihood reaches 0.9. Each record that joins adds its values and the rest are scored again until the joined records stop changing; a record whose likelihood falls below 0.9 is not joined and adds nothing, so a reviewer's rejection also undoes the joins that came through the rejected record. A record whose source kind starts with domain_ describes an email's domain, not the person, so it is not compared and adds nothing to what is known: it stays at 0.99 when the person's own email found it and at 0.5 otherwise.
